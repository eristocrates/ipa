The Binary Code
Any complex choice can be coded as a series of binary
choices, usually symbolized by 0 and 1.
You can calculate the number of binary digits or “bits” needed to code a set of
possibilities by taking the logarithm to the base 2 of the number of possibilities.
Thus with 26 letters and a space we have Log227 = 5 bits. Thus using the binary
either/or as our basic unit of information we can count the amount of
information by counting the number of bits it takes to code it.
Thus
A = 00000
B = 00001
C = 00010
D = 00011
E = 00100
F = 00101
Etc.
This is the basis of modern computing where the
computer is a chip with transistors that can be used
as a gate either open or closed. Thus the binary code
becomes a series of transistor positions. Computers
are programmed with Boolean logic.

set of possibilities there is often confusion about the difference. Some
people even think that information is a probability function. The
difference is that information is measured in bits and probabilities are
measured in fractions.

The term “cybernetics” comes from the Greek word
“ΚΥΒΕΡΝΑΩ” which means to steer, to guide or to
govern. 
In plain English it is the theory of control.
The study of information and control is called “cybernetics.” 

We are all familiar with the basic control process.
1. (ACT) You do something. 
2. (SENSE)  You see how it turned out. 
3. (EVALUATE) You compare the results to what you wanted. 
4. (CHOOSE) You choose another action to bring it closer to
your goal.
5. (ACT) You do something else.

Predicting the Future
Matter and energy are constants therefore
matter/ energy processes are predictable.
Information is a decision therefore information
processes are only partly predictable.
Programmed decisions are predictable, but the
program can be changed.
The future is not perfectly predictable because it
depends on decisions that haven’t been made yet.
